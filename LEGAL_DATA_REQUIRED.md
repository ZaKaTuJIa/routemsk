# Legal data required before publication

The privacy policy and consent cannot be finalized without:

1. Full legal name of the service provider.
2. Status: individual, self-employed, individual entrepreneur or company.
3. INN and, if applicable, OGRNIP/OGRN.
4. Contact email for personal-data requests.
5. Business/postal address when legally required.
6. Exact list of data received from clients.
7. Purpose and retention period for each data category.
8. Who can access documents and where they are stored.
9. Procedure and deadline for deletion on client request.
10. Contract/payment model and the party receiving money.

Until these fields are supplied, the site must not claim that its privacy
policy is complete, and automatic document upload must remain disabled.
